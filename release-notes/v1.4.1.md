## LitePPTX 1.4.1: updates you can't miss

A small release that makes the in-app updater behave like a polished app.

### What's new

- **A red dot on the ⋯ button** stays on until you update, so a pending update is always visible.
- **The menu entry adapts:** it reads *Update to v…* or *Install v…* and does that action directly.
- **Downloads are remembered.** Close the app, go offline or cancel Android's installer: the bar comes back as "ready to install" and nothing is downloaded twice. The APK is kept where cache clean-ups cannot delete it, and is removed automatically once the new version is installed.
- If the saved APK is ever gone, the button simply goes back to **Update** and downloads it again.
- After you allow installs from LitePPTX in Android settings, returning to the app continues the install.

### Good to know

- The update flow was tested on an Android emulator (detect, download, cancel the installer, restart offline, install from the menu). The final install step with the release key has not been tested on a real phone yet.

### Install

Download `LitePPTXViewer.apk` below, or tap **Update** in the app if you are on 1.3.1 or newer. The SHA-256 is in `LitePPTXViewer.apk.sha256`. Needs Android 5.0+.
