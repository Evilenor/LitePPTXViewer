## LitePPTX 1.3.1: crash fix

**If you installed v1.3.0, please update.** That build shipped without its main screen and could close right after launch. This release restores it, so install it over the top; your settings are kept.

### Fixed
- The viewer screen is back. v1.3.0 was published with an empty `MainActivity`, which made the app unusable.

### In-app updates, now exactly as documented
- Update bar at the top when a newer release exists; tap it to read *What's new*.
- **Update** downloads the APK with live progress, and the button turns into **Cancel** while it runs.
- Checks run at launch (at most every 6 hours while you are up to date) and on demand from the menu: **Check for updates**.
- The download is verified (size, SHA-256, package identity) before Android's installer opens.

### Install
Download `LitePPTXViewer.apk` below. The file's SHA-256 is in `LitePPTXViewer.apk.sha256`. Needs Android 5.0+.
