## LitePPTX 1.3.2: support button

A small release: LitePPTX now has a proper way to say thanks.

### What's new

- **Donate card** on the start screen: a pink-gradient card with a softly beating heart that opens the support page in your browser.
- **Donate ♥** entry in the menu (the three dots), available on every screen.
- English and Persian text for both.
- The app still sends nothing for this: the button just opens your browser.
- README (English and Persian) and release notes now carry the support link.
- *What's new* dialog: Markdown links are shown as plain text and the title is localized.

### Support LitePPTX ♥

LitePPTX is free, open source and ad-free. If it saves you time, a donation keeps it that way: **[the-evilenor.vercel.app/support](https://the-evilenor.vercel.app/support)**. It is also one tap away inside the app.

### Install

Download `LitePPTXViewer.apk` below, or tap **Update** in the app if you are on 1.3.1. The SHA-256 is in `LitePPTXViewer.apk.sha256`. Needs Android 5.0+.
