## LitePPTX 1.4.0: document scanner

LitePPTX can now scan paper. Still free, still ad-free, still no third-party libraries.

### What's new

- **Document scanner.** Tap **Scan document** on the start screen, in the menu (the three dots), or long-press the app icon.
  - Take photos with your phone's own camera app (LitePPTX needs **no camera permission**) or pick them from the gallery, several pages in a row.
  - The page edges are found automatically; drag the four corners if they are off. The page is straightened on your phone.
  - Filters: **Original**, **Clean** (removes shadows), **B&W** and **Gray**. Rotate any page.
  - **Create PDF**, then save to Downloads, open or share. The dialog stays open after *Save*, so you can save and then share.
- **Export as PDF for presentations:** **⋯ > Export as PDF** renders every slide to a PDF page.
- Everything runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded.

### Good to know

- No OCR yet (the PDF is images, not searchable text) and no ID-card mode.
- Automatic edge detection works best on a plain background. On a busy one, set the corners by hand.
- The scanner has been tested on an Android emulator, not yet on many real phones. If it misbehaves on yours, please [open an issue](https://github.com/Evilenor/LitePPTXViewer/issues).
- The APK is now about 131 KB (it was about 97 KB).

### Install

Download `LitePPTXViewer.apk` below, or tap **Update** in the app if you are on 1.3.1 or newer. The SHA-256 is in `LitePPTXViewer.apk.sha256`. Needs Android 5.0+.
