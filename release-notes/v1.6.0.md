## LitePPTX 1.6.0: Universal viewer, PDF tools and a Magic scan filter

Still zero dependencies, still tiny — now it opens almost any document you are sent, not just presentations.

### New features

- **Universal document viewer.** Open `.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.txt`, `.csv`, `.rtf` and `.md` from the file picker, the new *Open document* home tile, the overflow menu, or the *Share* menu of any app.
  - **Word:** styles and headings, bullets and numbering, tables, right-to-left text and inline images.
  - **Sheets:** sheet tabs, A/B/C column headers, column widths, cell colours, merged cells, Excel-style date/percent formatting and go-to-row.
  - **Plain text:** `.txt`, RFC 4180 `.csv` (first row bold), `.rtf` and Markdown with headings, bullets and inline `**bold**` / `*italic*` / `` `code` ``.
  - **Everywhere:** full-text search, text size 0.8–1.6×, pinch-to-zoom, share the original file, open externally, keep screen on.
- **Legacy Office, text-only.** Old `.doc`, `.ppt` and `.xls` files open as recovered plain text, with an honest note that formatting, tables and images are lost.
- **PDF tools.** A new *PDF Tools* screen (also on the home screen) with three offline tools:
  - **Compress:** tries a lossless rebuild first — it re-deflates the compressed streams inside classic PDFs and only keeps the result when it saves at least 5%. If that cannot help, it offers a rendered rebuild at Keep (200 dpi), Balanced (170 dpi) or Small (130 dpi) — and says plainly that pages become images.
  - **Merge:** pick two or more PDFs; they are joined in the order you picked them.
  - **Split:** pick a page range and get just those pages as a new PDF.
- **Magic scan filter.** A fifth scanner look for dull or faded pages: stronger levels, a saturation boost and a light sharpening pass.
- **Scan PDF quality choice.** Before building a scan PDF you now pick Original, Smart or Small (remembered for next time).
- **Save scanned pages as images.** From the scanner start screen, every captured page can go to Downloads as a JPEG.

### Improvements

- Recent files now cover documents and sheets too, with block/row counts instead of slide counts.
- The home screen gains a third tools row: **PDF Tools** and **Open document**.

### Technical

- APK size: still under ~250 KB (to verify from CI). Still no third-party libraries.
- New `office/` package: a shared OOXML zip/theme helper, DOCX and XLSX parsers (pure platform XML, no POI), an OLE2 text-recovery fallback and in-memory `.txt`/`.csv`/`.rtf`/`.md` parsers.
- New `pdf/PdfTools.kt`: a classic-PDF optimiser (fresh xref table, verified with `PdfRenderer`) plus rendered rebuilds through the existing `PdfBuilder`.
- XLSX is capped at 5,000 rows / 100 columns and DOCX at 2,000 blocks; the viewer says so when the cap is hit.

### Limitations (honest)

- Legacy `.doc`/`.ppt`/`.xls` are text-only; charts/SmartArt are still not rendered.
- Lossless PDF compression only works on classic xref-table PDFs. Most PDFs from scanners and office apps qualify; PDFs with object streams fall back to the rendered rebuild.
- The rendered rebuild turns pages into images, so text can no longer be selected or searched.
